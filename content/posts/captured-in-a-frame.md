  Wroxham railway bridge for our breakfast. Idyllic.

poem: |
  Monochrome the soft morning light
  as dawn sees the night take flight.

  The air is still, the waters are calm,
  nature embraces; the quiet is a balm.

  ​Gentle mists languidly drift, and in
  the golden hour, they gradually lift.

  ​Colour saturates with the sun,
  the day develops its daily hum.

  ​A moment in time forever claimed
  Captured in a single frame.
