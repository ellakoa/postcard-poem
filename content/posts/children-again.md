poem: |
  Children again that day on the beach
  Jumping the waves and bathing our feet.

  Holding hands and laughing out loud
  Just you and me alone in the crowd.

  Not caring who saw us, daft old folk
  Sharing life's most precious joke.

  The magic never goes away.
  Children again that summer's day.
