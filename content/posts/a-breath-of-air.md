poem: |
  A breath of air hardly there, passed me by 
  beneath a sun-bleached sky. Seeking shade, 
  a tree bade me welcome. Feeling weary, 
  I settled there.

  I fell into a languid slumber, dreaming of lands 
  yonder, with snow swirling all around, drifting 
  on a sun-parched ground.

  The lightest touch, gently teasing, drew me 
  from slumbered rest. Eyes bleary, I saw snow 
  falling - had my dreams manifest?

  I cupped a flake and smiled. Clearly, there 
  before my eyes, fluffy seedheads all about 
  me dancing on a breath of air. A breath of 
  air that was hardly there.
